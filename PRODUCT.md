# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shopify customers who want to understand how they participate in Meat For Free win actions, and the operator who needs the public embed and admin surfaces to feel trustworthy, readable, and controlled.

## Product Purpose

Meat For Free connects purchases and free-entry requests to lottery entries, shows live draw information in Shopify, and gives customers a clean path to view active win actions and their own dashboard. Success means users quickly understand how to participate without exposing private customer data.

## Brand Personality

Direct, premium, energetic. The product should feel like a real commerce utility with enough Meat For Free identity to stay recognizable, but never become noisy or unclear.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS decoration, fake complexity, over-styled dashboards, cluttered menus, and placeholder data that looks like confirmed real winners when no real winners exist.

## Design Principles

Show actionable information first.
Keep lottery trust and privacy visible.
Use brand assets and colors with restraint.
Design every widget as a Shopify embed first: compact, responsive, and readable.
Prefer scalable reusable components over one-off blocks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, readable Dutch copy, reduced-motion support, and fallbacks when external media fails.
